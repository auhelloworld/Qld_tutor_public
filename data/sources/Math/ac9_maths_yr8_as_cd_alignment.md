# Year 8 Mathematics: Australian Curriculum Version 9.0
## Achievement Standard Aligned to Content Descriptions (ACIQ v9.0)

**Source:** Queensland Curriculum & Assessment Authority (QCAA)

## Overview
This document outlines the alignment between specific aspects of the Year 8 Mathematics achievement standard and the corresponding content descriptions under the Australian Curriculum (AC) Version 9.0.
